## K. KEY DESIGN INSIGHTS FROM THE AUDIT (no calculations yet)

1. **It is a 4-column table-top structure with 3.5 m cantilevers on all sides.** The corner cantilevers (≈ 4.95 m diagonal) and wind uplift on a free-standing canopy are expected to govern member sizing, base-plate anchors and footing size. Gravity alone will likely not govern.
2. **The 750 mm façade limit is the single most important geometric constraint.** It caps the structural depth (if A1 holds), and that pushes the scheme towards shallow, stiff, steel-intensive systems: a two-way beam grillage, a box-girder ring, or a shallow plane/space truss.
3. **Lateral stability comes only from the four columns** unless knee-braces or tree-branch struts are introduced. These shorten cantilevers and spans but must keep the 6.25 m clearance and circulation.
4. **Coastal plus cyclonic** means durability and wind-uplift detailing (anchors, sheet fixings, connections) carry real marks and real risk.
5. **A splice and a 2D frame analysis are mandatory** even if the scheme does not naturally need them. Plan them in from Phase 1.
